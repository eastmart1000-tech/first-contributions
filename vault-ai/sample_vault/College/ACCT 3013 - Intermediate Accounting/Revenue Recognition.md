# Revenue Recognition (ASC 606)

## Five steps
1. Identify the contract with a customer
2. Identify the performance obligations
3. Determine the transaction price
4. Allocate the transaction price to the performance obligations
5. Recognize revenue when (or as) each obligation is satisfied

## Example
Software license + 1 year support sold for $1,200. Standalone prices: license $1,000, support $400.
Allocate by relative standalone selling price: license $857, support $343 (recognized monthly).
